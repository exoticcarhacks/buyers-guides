# Lexus LC500 2018-2026 Buyer Guide

## Introduction
The Lexus LC500 is a naturally aspirated 5.0L V8 grand-touring coupe that blends distinctive styling with a luxurious driving experience. This guide covers the V8 model only. The LC500h hybrid is omitted. The LC500 is a strong hack candidate due to its exceptional reliability record, relatively low depreciation compared to European rivals, and the enduring appeal of its high-revving V8. The main risk lies in navigating the various packages and options to ensure optimal resale value and avoid less desirable configurations.

## Known Problems and Recalls

### Known Problems

| Issue | What To Know/Check |
|---|---|
| Fuel Pump Failure | May stop operating, leading to vehicle stall. Check if recall 20V-012 or 20V-682 has been performed. |
| Backup Camera Condensation | Intermittent condensation buildup inside the lens. Check camera functionality and clarity. |
| HVAC Servo Motor Malfunction | Requires cleaning, regreasing, or replacement of servo motors. Listen for unusual noises from the HVAC system or check for inconsistent temperature control. |
| Roof Body Creak | Requires adding shims or C-Pillar work. Listen for creaking noises from the roof area, especially over uneven surfaces. |
| Popping Noises from B Pillar | Requires removing back seat, adding welds, and greasing bolts. Listen for popping sounds from the B-pillar area. |
| Bolts coming off the Rear Spoiler | Requires re-securing or replacing bolts. Visually inspect the rear spoiler for looseness or missing bolts. |
| Door Handle Actuator Failure | Actuator that pops in and out fails. Test door handle operation multiple times. |
| Transmission Clunkiness | At lower speeds and normal driving. Pay close attention to transmission shifts during a test drive. |
| Squeaky Performance Brake Pads | When pulling out. Listen for brake squeal during low-speed maneuvers. |

## Conclusion

The Lexus LC500 V8 is a strong hack candidate with an exceptional reliability record and mild depreciation relative to European rivals. The minimum year to target is 2021, which brought the most significant platform updates including improved suspension, chassis refinements, brake-based vectoring, and Android Auto. The 2022 and 2023 models are virtually the same as 2021 with minor suspension tweaks and are equally strong buys. For buyers who prioritize modern technology and a touchscreen interface, the 2024 is the right choice as it replaced the widely criticized Remote Touch touchpad with a proper 12.3-inch touchscreen. Target well-optioned examples with the Dynamic Handling Package and Mark Levinson audio. Inspiration Series examples are the most collectible and command the strongest premiums.
